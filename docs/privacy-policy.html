BE Benchmark Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

BE Benchmark is an offline CPU benchmarking app published by Amandeep. This policy describes information the app uses and where it is stored.

Information stored by the app

To show your results and history, BE Benchmark stores completed scores, test mode and suite version, run date and duration, device model, and raw performance measurements such as test timings, throughput, thread count, CPU clock readings, and available temperature readings. The app also stores your local preferences, including core mode, theme, and temperature unit.

Live CPU load is read from Android while the app is open so the core graphs can be drawn. Live load is not included in saved benchmark records. The app does not request your name, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, or device serial number.

How information is used and shared

Benchmark workloads, including bundled AI inference models, run locally on your device. BE Benchmark has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated result service. The app has no Internet permission and does not send your benchmark results or model inputs to Amandeep or a BE Benchmark server.

Storage, backup, export, and deletion

Saved runs and preferences are kept in the app's private storage. Android device backup may include app data depending on your Android and device backup settings. Backups and restores are handled by Android and the backup service you choose.

You may export results as a JSON file using Android's document picker. The file is written to the destination you select; that destination may be local or provided by a cloud service, and its own privacy terms apply. Use Clear Result History in the app's Settings to delete saved runs from the app. Exported files and existing Android backups are not deleted by that action. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Children

BE Benchmark is a general-purpose device performance utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
